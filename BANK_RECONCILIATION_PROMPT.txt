Amazon Refund Ledger bank reconciliation request

Upload the exported bank-credit-check request JSON to ChatGPT and ask it to reconcile every return against connected Finances accounts.

Rules:
- Only posted credits can confirm Bank credited.
- Pending transactions may be reported as pending but are not confirmed.
- Prefer exact refund amount, matching card last four when available, Amazon merchant evidence, and a plausible posting date after refund issuance.
- Never reuse one credit for multiple return records unless it clearly represents a combined refund.
- Missing exact evidence stays pending/not_found; multiple plausible matches are ambiguous.
- Do not include bank credentials, full account numbers, or unrelated transactions in the result.
